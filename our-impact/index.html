Our impact

Twenty years. Hundreds of lives. One continuing mission.

Since 2004, Aai Samajsevi Sanstha has worked to give vulnerable children shelter, education, food, healthcare and a chance to grow. The measure is not only how many children were supported — it is the lives they build after Balgram.

Hundreds of vulnerable children have received shelter, education, food and medical support over more than two decades.

What they go on to become

Former residents have gone on to become software engineers, police officers, PWD engineers, social workers, management professionals, fashion designers and entrepreneurs.

Wherever it is safe and appropriate, Aai works to strengthen families and reunite children with them — through investigation, counselling and mediation. More than 70 such reunifications have been reported.

Every number has a story.

The true impact of Aai is not measured only by buildings, programmes or statistics. It is measured by lives that change direction.

  • A child who learns to read.
  • A student who enters college.
  • A young adult who earns independently.
  • A former resident who starts a family.
  • A child who discovers a talent.
  • A family that is reunited.
  • A young person who returns to give back.

These are the stories that define Balgram. We do not invent named testimonials. The lives above are the pattern the organisation reports.

A year in the life of the family — 2024–25

The 2024–25 annual report records a year of health camps, field visits, sports, cultural celebrations, cleanliness initiatives and the continuing development of Yuvagram.

Balgram children won 8 first prizes, 3 second prizes and 1 third prize at the district-level Chacha Nehru Children’s Festival — across sports, arts, writing and other competitions. They also achieved district-level success in badminton and yoga.

Educational visits that year included the Georai Municipal Council, waste-management and water-purification facilities, the Georai Court and the Police Station.

A health camp provided eye and health examinations, medicines, and glasses where they were required.
