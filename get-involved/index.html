Support a Child

Get involved

You can become part of a child's journey.

Aai Samajsevi Sanstha relies on the compassion of individuals, organisations and well-wishers. Support is invited with respect — not with guilt. Give. Visit. Volunteer. Partner.

Together, we can help every child find a family, discover their potential and build a dignified future.

DonateVisit or write

Sponsor a child

Walk with one child through the ordinary and essential things: a home, school, healthcare and the space to grow. Sponsorship is not charity from a distance. It is how a child keeps a place in the family.

Sponsor a meal

A shared table is part of what makes Balgram a family. Your contribution can help provide nutritious food — the daily act that holds children together.

Support education

Help with school expenses, books, stationery, technology and the chance to learn beyond the classroom. Education is where independence begins.

Support higher education

Young adults at Yuvagram are already in college and working toward standing on their own. Supporting higher education keeps that path open after 18.

Support infrastructure

Contribute toward residential facilities, educational spaces and other essential buildings that make a family life possible.

Donate materials

The organisation welcomes school supplies, books, toys, food grains, groceries, computers, furniture, bicycles and construction materials.

Volunteer

Share time, knowledge, skill or experience with the children and young adults. Presence matters as much as money.

CSR partnership

Organisations can partner to support education, healthcare, infrastructure, vocational training, sustainability and other social initiatives. Named partners are listed only when confirmed.

Visit Balgram

The best way to understand the work is to meet the family. You are warmly invited to Balgram in Georai, Beed, and to connect with Yuvagram in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar.

A child’s future is not fixed at birth.

For more than two decades Aai has worked so that abandonment can become a family, and a childhood can become an independent life. You can be part of that journey.
